How Reins works

The owner's money sits in a NEAR contract vault. Each agent gets a rein: rules bound to one public key that the owner adds as a function-call access key. Every payment is checked by the contract before anything moves.
1

Fund the vault

Deposit NEAR, or tUSD with ft_transfer_call. Only your full-access key can withdraw.

2

Hand a key

Describe the limit in plain English. Reins creates the rein and adds a function-call key that can call only pay, pay_evm, request_approval, issue_subrein.

3

Agent pays

Claude, Codex or any MCP agent calls pay. The guard screens the receiver and the prompt; the contract checks budget, cap, merchants, blocklist and expiry along the whole rein tree.

4

You stay in charge

Big payments wait for your approval. Freeze or revoke in one click. On other chains, NEAR Chain Signatures sign only what the rein allows.
